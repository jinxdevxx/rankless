# Rankless

From E-Rank to legend. A browser tower-defense / lane-battle incremental game.

You are the weakest Breaker in the city. Clear rifts, bind the monsters you defeat, build your legion and climb the ranks.

**Play:** https://jinxdevxx.github.io/rankless/

## Features (prototype v0.1)
- Create your champion: codename + one of 4 Affinities (Crimson Flame, Storm, Void, Blood)
- Lane battles: your champion fights alongside summoned units against the Rift Core
- Territory mechanic: push the front line to earn more Essence
- Bind system: defeat enough monsters of a kind and they join your legion
- Awakening ultimate, Seals (base turrets), affinity skills
- District 1 "Old Harbor": 5 rifts + Rift Break boss (Harbor Warden)
- Stats, level ups, Black Market gear, permanent Contract upgrades
- Daily Directives and idle Patrol income (also offline)
- Auto-save in the browser

## Controls
- `1`-`9` summon units · `Q` affinity skill · `R` Awakening · `Space` Advance/Hold · `P` pause
